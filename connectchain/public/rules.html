Connect Chain Rules


Words

  • Things like swearing, profanity, and hate speech will not be tolerated.
      Also will most likley end in a 1-10 day ban, and if you continue after that, you may face a permanent ban.

  • If you spam, you may face a temporary or permanent ban depending on the severity and frequency of the offense.

  • If you swear in chatrooms, while playing, and etc, you will most likley face a warning depending on the severity.
      • ANY RACIAL OR DEREAGATORY SLURS WILL RESULT IN A PERMANENT BAN, OR ANY COMMENTS MAKING FUN OF ETHNIC MINORITIES WILL RESULT IN A PERMANENT BAN. If you see someone using such language, please report it to moderators.

Moderator Harassment

  • Comments that harass moderators will not be tolerated. If you have a problem with a moderator, please contact the support team instead of harassing them in chatrooms or other public areas.
      • Harassment of moderators may result in a temporary or permanent ban depending on the severity and frequency of the offense.

  • Any begging or pleading to have special treatment will not be tolerated.
      • For example, begging the owner or moderators for power or privileges such as more XP, or begging for moderator status constantly. Remember that you are allowed to ask for moderator status if you are respectful about it.

  • Any comments that are disrespectful to moderators will not be tolerated.
      • For example, calling moderators names, saying they make stupid decisions, or making fun of them in any way.
    • Remember, the moderators are here to help maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for all users.

    Exploitation

    • Using bugs, glitches, exploits, scripts, bots, or hacks is not allowed.
        • If reported or seen using any of these, you may face a very drastic ban, or permanent ban.

    • If you see exploits being used, please report them immediately to the moderators.

      • Account Rules

      • Sharing accounts is at your own risk. Each user must be aware of the consequences.
          • Sharing accounts is highly discouraged!

      • Account trading is not allowed.
          • If you are caught trading accounts, you may face a permanent ban.
      • Impersonation of other users is not allowed.
          • If you are caught impersonating another user, you may face a warning to change your name, and if you continue the offense, you may face a permanent ban.

        Other Rules

        • Do not submit fake reports or intentionally waste staff time.

        • Please have appropriate usernames, you cannot have usernames that are inappropriate or offensive.
            • Inappropriate usernames, include any political figures or relations, any swear words, or sexual words may result in a warning or temporary ban, or even a permanent ban to the extent of the offense.
        • Do not post any personal information about yourself or others.
            • Posting personal information about yourself or others may result in a permanent ban.
        • Do not send any links, whatsoever, or spam advertisements to social media.

          • Reminder that this game is for fun and not meant for serious or harmful activities.
